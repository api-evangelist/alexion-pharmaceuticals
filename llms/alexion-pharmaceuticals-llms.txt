# Alexion Pharmaceuticals

> Alexion Pharmaceuticals was a global biopharmaceutical company founded in 1992 and headquartered in Boston, Massachusetts, focused on life-transforming therapies for rare diseases. It pioneered complement inhibition therapy (Soliris/eculizumab, Ultomiris/ravulizumab). AstraZeneca acquired Alexion in July 2021 for $39 billion; it now operates as Alexion, AstraZeneca Rare Disease. Alexion publishes no public developer API; its public technical surface is a GitHub organization (alxndgb) of rare-disease research tools.
